Visitor Map

Explore website visits by country, state or province, and city.

I set up this Visitor Map to get a simple sense of where visitors to my website are coming from.

It provides a geographic view of website traffic by country or region, state or province, and city, making it easy to see how the site reaches people across different places over time.

The map is mainly for curiosity and perspective. As I share research, notes, and small tools online, I find it interesting to see where visitors come from and how the audience gradually expands.

It is a simple feature, but I like the idea of being able to visualize how something shared online can reach people in different parts of the world.
