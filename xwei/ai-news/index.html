Daily AI News Briefing

A daily AI news briefing connecting developments to research — accumulating knowledge and keeping pace with the field.

I built the Daily AI News Briefing to help me keep up with the rapidly evolving AI landscape in a more structured and research-oriented way.

Each briefing brings together the day's key AI developments, including article-level analysis, cross-article synthesis, and potential research directions. The goal is to provide a coherent view of what is happening, rather than simply presenting a collection of individual news stories, so you can get the broader picture in one morning read.

It also helps me build cumulative knowledge around relevant theories, concepts, and research questions. Over time, the morning reading becomes more than staying informed—it becomes a way to develop understanding that can be carried directly into research.

If you find it useful, I'd be very happy to hear your feedback, suggestions, or ideas for improvement.
