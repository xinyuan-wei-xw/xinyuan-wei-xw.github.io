I build small tools to make my own work and everyday life a little easier: staying organized, planning meals, and occasionally making academia a little more fun.

Xinyuan's Toolbox brings these projects together in one place. Some are practical tools I use regularly, while others are experiments, learning resources, or small side projects built around problems I wanted to solve for myself. They are all free to use.

I'm sharing them in case they are useful to others as well. If you try any of them, please feel free to contact me with feedback, suggestions, or ideas for improvement. I'd be very happy to hear from you.

AI use note. These tools were built with AI assistance. X Planner’s optional AI tasks feature (Google Gemini) and MockRoom’s optional cloud voice (OpenAI) run only when you turn them on, using API keys you provide yourself and keep on your own device. Eat Well has no AI features.
