Cloud neural voice (optional)

Browser voices can only go so far. For a genuinely softer, more human read-aloud, MockRoom can use OpenAI's neural speech instead — generated fresh for each question, with natural pauses and intonation. It is off by default and completely optional; the local browser voice above keeps working either way.

Off · questions are read by your browser voice
When on, the cloud voice replaces the browser voice for reading and previews
The key is stored only in this browser's local storage, on this device. It is never saved into MockRoom, never sent anywhere except to OpenAI itself (as the sign-in for the speech request), and you can remove it here at any time. Speech usage is billed to your own OpenAI account.
Please note — what leaves this device: while cloud voice is on, the text of each question (and of any preview) is sent from your browser directly to OpenAI to be turned into speech. Nothing else goes: your answers, recordings, uploaded files, and timing data stay on this device, exactly as before. If you'd rather nothing leave the device at all, leave cloud voice off and use the browser voice.

Tip: the coach starts listening once you tap “Start answer”. A pace of 120–160 words per minute is easiest to follow; leave a small pause between sentences and let your pitch rise and fall — it sounds more confident.

The coach analyses sound only (pitch, rhythm, pauses); it never transcribes or uploads anything — so it can't hear filler words like “um” and “ah”. Count those yourself when you replay your recording.
